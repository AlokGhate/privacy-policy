Privacy policy — Image to PDF: Private

Effective and last updated: November 1, 2026

Image to PDF: Private (“the app”) turns photos and screenshots into PDF files on your Android phone. It is built so that your images never leave your device unless you choose to share them. This policy explains what the app handles, what stays on your phone, and the few cases where data reaches anyone else.

In short: the app has no internet permission, no ads, no analytics and no account. The developer never receives your images or PDFs. The developer receives something from you only if you choose to email it.

What the app does with your images

  • You choose the images, either with Android’s photo picker or by sharing them to the app from another app. The app can read only the images you give it.
  • Images are converted on your phone. The app has no internet permission, so it cannot upload them.
  • Your page list exists only while the app is running. It is never saved, so it is gone once the app is closed.
  • The PDF goes only where you send it: a location you pick with Android’s file picker, or an app you choose in the share sheet. Once you send it elsewhere, that app or service handles it under its own terms.
  • The app turns off Android cloud backup and device-to-device transfer for its data.

What the app stores on your phone

All of this stays in the app’s private storage. None of it is sent to the developer.

  • Temporary files. Working files are kept while a PDF is being made and deleted when the export finishes. A PDF made for sharing stays until the next time the app starts, so that the app you shared it with can finish reading it. Every app start clears any that remain.
  • Your settings. Your theme, your default page size, and your crash report choice.
  • Usage counters. How many free exports you used today, how many exports you have made in total (used only to decide when to ask for a review), and whether you have answered the notification question.
  • Your Pro status. A note of whether you own Pro.
  • The save in progress. While a PDF is being saved, the app keeps a link to the file it is writing, so it can clean up a partial file if it is interrupted. The link is removed when the save ends.

Data collected by the developer

The app itself sends nothing to the developer. The developer receives personal data in only two cases, and both start with you sending an email:

  • a crash report you choose to send (see below);
  • a message you send to the contact address below.

In both cases the developer receives your email address and whatever the email contains. This information is used only to fix problems and answer you. It is not sold, not used for advertising, and not shared with anyone, except as needed for email delivery or if required by law.

Crash reports (optional)

Crash reports are off by default. If you turn on “Offer to send crash reports” in Settings, the app asks you after each crash. If you agree, the report opens in your own email app, so you can read it before sending, or not send it.

A report contains the error’s technical details (a stack trace), the app version, the Android version, and the phone’s model and brand. It is designed to never contain images, file names or PDFs.

Reports travel as ordinary email, so your email provider and the developer’s email provider handle them, and how they are protected in transit depends on those providers.

Google Play

  • Purchases. Pro is a one-time purchase handled by Google Play. The app only asks Google Play whether you own Pro, and never sees your payment details.
  • Reviews. After a few exports, the app may ask Google Play to show its review prompt. Google Play decides whether to show it and handles any review you leave.

The app reaches Google Play through the Play Store app on your phone. Google handles that data under Google’s privacy policy.

Permissions

  • Notifications: optional; used only to show export progress and a Cancel button.
  • Foreground service: keeps an export running while you use another app.

The app does not request internet, camera, location, contacts or all-files access.

Keeping and deleting your data

  • On your phone: you can delete everything the app stores by clearing its storage in Android Settings or by uninstalling it. This resets your settings and today’s export count. Pro is not lost: it is restored from Google Play the next time the app starts, on the same Google account.
  • Emails to the developer: these are kept only as long as needed to fix the problem or answer you, then deleted. To have your email deleted sooner, or to ask what the developer holds about you, write to the contact address below.

Security

Your data is protected by staying on your phone, in app storage that other apps cannot read. If you share a PDF or send it elsewhere, its protection depends on where you send it.

Children

The app is not directed at children. It does not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child has emailed the developer, write to the contact address below and the email will be deleted.
